# Three-minute demo

1. Open <https://hydratrace.vercel.app> and show that the engine is online.
2. In **New scan**, import the three Acme Commerce fixture lockfiles.
3. Create the compromised package incident and open **Incident center**.
4. Show exact affected services, three production paths, safe controls, and evidence refs.
5. Open **Timeline replay** to compare current and historical exposure.
6. Show static/runtime/unknown reachability and package-neighborhood reasons.
7. Generate a remediation, compare the lockfile diff, and emphasize that verification
   passes only after a strong zero-path HydraDB query.
8. Ask the copilot a question, then point out that the graph decides and AI only explains.
9. Open **How HydraDB is used** and show the recorded persistence/indexer/restart gate.
